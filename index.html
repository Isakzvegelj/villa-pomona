The Villa

Your Peaceful Escape

Villa Pomona is nestled in Bled's finest neighbourhood of historic villas — a tranquil oasis away from city stress and noise, yet minutes from everything Bled offers.

Ideal for couples seeking relaxation, the villa sits on a quiet street lined with beautiful heritage homes. Inside, you'll find three thoughtfully appointed bedrooms, a cosy lounge library, and an outside common area balcony perfect for morning coffee or evening wine.

The property features a private swimming pool set in lovely gardens, a sauna for relaxation, a separate pool house with its own charm, and parking for up to five cars. All guests have full access to the pool and sauna. A convenience store, pharmacy, sports facilities, and the town centre are all within easy walking distance. Staff support is available on demand to ensure a seamless stay.

Every bathroom at Villa Pomona features genuine Slovene marble sourced from the Pohorje region — a rare and elite material renowned for its elegant, prestigious appearance.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 10:00. Early check-in and late check-out may be available on request, subject to availability. Direct bookings receive complimentary late check-out when possible.

Is breakfast included? Can I get drinks?

Breakfast is available on request and can be arranged during your stay. We also offer drinks upon request. Please let us know your preferences when booking or during your stay.

When is the swimming pool open?

The pool is seasonal and typically open from May to September, depending on weather conditions. It is not heated. Children must be supervised at all times. Exact opening dates are confirmed at the time of booking.

Is parking available?

Yes, Villa Pomona offers free private parking on-site for up to five vehicles. This is rare in central Bled and a great convenience for guests arriving by car.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes, we can arrange airport transfers from Ljubljana Jože Pučnik Airport (LJU, €60) or Klagenfurt Airport (KLU, €90). Select your preference in the booking form, and we'll confirm the details.

Is the villa suitable for children?

Yes, children are welcome. The property has a garden, pool (with supervision), and plenty of space for families. Please let us know if you have specific needs, and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Can I book individual bedrooms or the whole villa?

You can book individual bedrooms (101 — Deluxe Double, 102 — King Suite, or 103 — Queen Suite), the separate Pool House, or the entire Villa Pomona (all three bedrooms + pool house) for up to 8 guests. Each option offers a different experience.

Is there staff on site?

Staff support is available on demand to assist with anything you need during your stay. We're just a phone call or message away to ensure a seamless experience.
