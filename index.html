Location

The Best of Bled

Villa Pomona is perfectly positioned in Bled's most desirable neighbourhood — a quiet enclave of historic villas, yet close to everything. Just steps from the beautiful Lake Bled.

The villa is situated on a tranquil street lined with beautiful heritage homes, offering peace and privacy while being just a short walk from Lake Bled, the town centre, and all attractions.

📍

Walking Distance

Lake Bled (3 min), town centre (5 min), convenience store (2 min), pharmacy (3 min), sports facilities (4 min)

Nearby Attractions

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 10:00. Early check-in and late check-out may be available on request, subject to availability.

When is the swimming pool open?

The pool is seasonal and typically open from May to September, depending on weather conditions. It is not heated.

Is parking available?

Yes, Villa Pomona offers free private parking on-site for up to five vehicles.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes, we can arrange airport transfers from Ljubljana Airport (LJU) or Klagenfurt Airport (KLU). Prices are provided on request.

Can I book individual bedrooms or the whole villa?

You can book individual bedrooms, the separate Pool House, or the entire Villa Pomona for up to 8 guests.
