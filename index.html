The Villa

Your Peaceful Escape

Villa Pomona is nestled in Bled's finest neighbourhood of historic villas — a tranquil oasis away from city stress and noise, yet minutes from everything Bled offers.

Ideal for couples seeking relaxation, the villa sits on a quiet street lined with beautiful heritage homes. Inside, you'll find three thoughtfully appointed bedrooms, a cosy lounge library, and an outside common area balcony perfect for morning coffee or evening wine.

The property features a private swimming pool set in lovely gardens, a separate pool house with its own charm, and parking for up to five cars. A convenience store, pharmacy, sports facilities, and the town centre are all within easy walking distance. Staff support is available on demand to ensure a seamless stay.

Location

The Best of Bled

Villa Pomona is perfectly positioned in Bled's most desirable neighbourhood — a quiet enclave of historic villas, yet close to everything.

The villa is situated on a tranquil street lined with beautiful heritage homes, offering peace and privacy while being just a short walk from Lake Bled, the town centre, and all attractions.

📍

Walking Distance

Lake Bled (3 min), town centre (5 min), convenience store (2 min), pharmacy (3 min), sports facilities (4 min)
