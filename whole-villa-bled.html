Whole villa or individual rooms?

Choose the whole villa when your group wants the property to itself and access to the full kitchen. Choose an individual room or the Pool House when you do not need the entire property and are happy to share the pool and common facilities with other guests.

Plan around the season

The outdoor pool is typically open from May to September, depending on the weather. It is not heated. If swimming is essential to your stay, ask about pool availability for your dates before confirming.

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 10:00. Early arrival or late departure is subject to availability.
