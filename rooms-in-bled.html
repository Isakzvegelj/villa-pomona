Pool, sauna and practical details

The outdoor pool is seasonal, typically open from May to September depending on the weather, and is not heated. Sauna access and on-site parking are included.

When rooms are booked separately, the pool and shared facilities are used by other villa guests too. The full kitchen is available exclusively with a whole-villa booking.

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 10:00. For your dates and current rates, use the individual room calendars on our availability page.
